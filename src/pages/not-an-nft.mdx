# This is Not an NFT

## Why We're Anti-Crypto

Cryptocurrency and NFTs represent a fundamental misalignment of incentives. They:

- **Harm the environment** through excessive energy consumption
- **Enable scams** that prey on vulnerable people
- **Create artificial scarcity** where none needs to exist
- **Benefit the wealthy** at the expense of regular people

## Our Stance

We will never:

- Mint NFTs
- Accept cryptocurrency
- Partner with web3 companies
- Promote blockchain technology
