# Our Ethos

## Why We Exist

coomer.org is a community-driven platform dedicated to curating and sharing memes that bring joy and laughter. We believe in the power of humor to connect people and provide a moment of relief in our daily lives.

## Our Values

### Community First

We prioritize our community above all else. Every feature we build, every decision we make, is with our community in mind.

### Quality Over Quantity

We carefully curate content to ensure only the best memes make it to our platform. No spam, no low-effort content.

### Transparency

We're open about how we operate, how we fund the platform, and what we're working on next.

## Supporting Us

If you enjoy what we do, consider supporting us on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/coomerorg). Monthly supporters get access to exclusive video memes!

## Join Our Discord

Connect with fellow meme enthusiasts on our [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/pFvDZms3AY). It's where we share updates, take suggestions, and just hang out.
